About

With over 18 years of experience across B2B and B2C marketing, I specialise in performance strategy, marketing technology, and paid media execution. Currently Head of Marketing Technology at We Are Crank, I help brands scale sustainably and profitably in competitive markets.

My background spans the full growth stack — from advanced conversion tracking and server-side data infrastructure to creative paid media strategy across Meta, Google, and LinkedIn. I've led cross-functional developer teams as CTO, rebuilt mobile platforms from scratch, and worked with ecommerce brands, high-growth startups, and businesses raising seed to Series D funding.

Away from screens, I serve as Treasurer for Farmborough Pre School and previously sat on Farmborough Parish Council — a reflection of my belief that good strategy, in any context, starts with understanding your community.
